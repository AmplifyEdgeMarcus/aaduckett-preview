Our story

From ice boxes in 1919 to 25-story rooftop lifts.

Albert A. Duckett started selling ice boxes at 21, then grew into refrigeration, plumbing, heating and air conditioning. In 1973 the company joined the Elliott-Lewis family of companies, and in 1990 moved to its current home in Glassboro, NJ.

Today our team still does the heavy lifting. Watch an Aon unit ride a crane onto Independence Place in Philadelphia’s Society Hill, with lift help from affiliate Sautter Crane.
